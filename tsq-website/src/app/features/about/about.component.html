Who We Are

A Vibrant, Inclusive Community for Fearless Women

Tanzania Safari Queens is a vibrant, inclusive community for women of all backgrounds, races, and cultures who love Adventure, Connecting, and exploring the beauty of Tanzania and beyond.

We are a royal sisterhood of adventurous, bold, and fearless women. Here, we share, inspire, empower, and explore - from the heart of Tanzania to the world. Whether you're chasing dreams, discovering new places, growing a business, shaping your career, or finding your voice - you belong here.

We believe that adventure & success is better together.
